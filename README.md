**Rust · Docker · OpenTelemetry · Prometheus · Grafana · Loki · Tempo**

Developed a satellite simulator in collaboration with the **Libre Space Foundation**, focused on autonomous energy management in Low Earth Orbit.

Implemented a **Finite State Machine** for energy management and an observability stack for monitoring the system through metrics, logs and traces.

---

### ✨ A-Plataforma

**Java · Jakarta EE · Vue.js · AI · Apache PDFBox**

Full-stack publication management platform developed during my degree.
